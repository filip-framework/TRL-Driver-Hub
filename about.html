What we're about

We run structured, multi-tier championships with a simple promise: hard racing, fair stewarding and a community that welcomes newcomers as much as veterans.

  • Clean racing first. An independent stewards' panel reviews every reported incident against a published penalty scale.
  • Every race broadcast. Race nights are streamed with commentary so your results are always on record.
  • Room to progress. Promotion and relegation between tiers keeps the racing close and gives everyone something to fight for.
