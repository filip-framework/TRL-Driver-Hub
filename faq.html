Getting in

How do I sign in?

Use Sign in with Discord in the header. Your league roles (Team Principal, Race Control, driver) come from the Discord server. You never pick a role on the site.

Where is this FAQ after I sign in?

Click your name and avatar in the top-right. FAQ is in that menu, next to Team Principal (if you have the role) and Sign out.

Can I add this to my phone’s home screen?

Yes. It opens like its own app, with the league mark as the icon. On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.

I race, but I’m not a Team Principal. What do I use?

Join F1 to get on the driver list. After that, F1 home, standings, schedule, teams and your driver profile are the public championship. Endurance sign-up is separate under Endurance.

What’s live now

The F1 championship is the flagship. The public site runs like a league broadcast; Team Principals get a private office on top of that.

  • F1 championship hub — next race, title fight, driver directory, constructor marks.
  • Standings & calendar — drivers and constructors by division, lights-out in your local time.
  • Teams & contracts — roster, salary cap used and remaining, constructor logos.
  • Driver profiles — nation, number, licence status, season stats and race history.
  • Rulebook — sporting, financial and stewarding regulations on the site.
  • Race analysis — positions gained, gaps and penalty time for every published round.
  • Live draft — constructor night and driver night with a public board plus a Team Principal pick room.
  • Team Principal office — your constructor’s seats, cap, signings, reserves, waivers and draft entry.
  • Endurance garage — event calendar, I’m interested, cars and line-ups, plus fuel, stints and prep on the race desk.
  • Discord — still the clubhouse. The site is the source of truth for the grid.

Team Principals

Where is my team office?

Sign in, then open Team Principal from your profile menu. You’ll see the constructor linked to your Discord team role: division seats, remaining cap, draft buttons and roster actions.

How do I sign, reserve or waive a driver?

In the office, pick the division seat, then the action:

  • Request full-time signing — contract position is calculated from the salary table. Race Control may need to approve it.
  • Sign one-race reserve — a fixed fee, tied to one race, then it expires.
  • Waive — drops the driver and applies the waiver fee to the cap.

Pending requests show on your cap panel until Race Control decides. Going over the cap is allowed, but it costs constructors’ points at the end of the season. The rulebook has the brackets.

Do I still use Discord commands?

You can. /sign, /waive, /reserve and /cap still work for the constructor you lead. The website is the easier place to see the whole picture before you commit.

Draft night

Two sessions: constructors first, then drivers. Everyone can watch. Only the Team Principal on the clock can pick.

How do I get into the room?

From Team Principal, hit Enter Draft Room when Race Control opens the session.

What does the public board show?
